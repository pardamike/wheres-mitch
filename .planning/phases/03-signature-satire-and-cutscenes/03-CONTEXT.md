# Phase 3 Context: Signature Satire And Cutscenes

## Boundary

Replace functional placeholders with the final original visual language and the two signature
outcomes: Turtle Mitch returning to the Capitol after a catch, and the absurd helicopter escape
after ten misses. This phase also owns audio, controls, published copy, and the asset manifest.

## Locked Decisions

- **D3-01 — Original satire:** all visible art is original vector work or explicitly compatible
  licensed material. No Where's Waldo title treatment, stripes, composition, or copied artwork.
- **D3-02 — Turtle caricature:** Mitch reads through an exaggerated original head, dark suit cues,
  green turtle body, articulated legs, and a shell that remains identifiable at game scale.
- **D3-03 — Respectful spouse depiction:** Elaine Chao is a recognizable but non-grotesque
  original caricature in the cockpit; race and ethnicity are not the visual joke.
- **D3-04 — Clear national marker:** the helicopter carries a legible, correctly arranged PRC
  flag motif using the documented red/yellow colors; it is a cartoon prop, not factual evidence.
- **D3-05 — Nonviolent win:** Mitch freezes, reacts, retracts, and his shell travels through an
  absurd patriotic pneumatic route to a cartoon Capitol. No injury or weapon imagery.
- **D3-06 — Nonviolent loss:** money bags pop into existence, helicopter enters, rope lifts Mitch
  and bags, and everything exits. No crash, threat, restraint, or physical harm.
- **D3-07 — Outcome locks:** cutscene start synchronously blocks stage input; game-over restart is
  enabled only after the loss animation finishes.
- **D3-08 — Skippable presentation:** outcome sequences can be fast-forwarded with a labeled
  control after their essential result is communicated; reduced motion uses abbreviated staging.
- **D3-09 — Audio after gesture only:** Web Audio is created/resumed by Start or another explicit
  player gesture. Sounds are procedural or original bundled assets only.
- **D3-10 — Satire copy stays fictional:** copy describes impossible game events, never alleges
  real conduct, health status, absence, finances, or foreign ties.

## Canonical Outcome Beats

### Catch

1. Freeze the scene and dim the crowd.
2. Spotlight Mitch and display **FOUND HIM!**
3. Mitch reacts; head retracts into the shell.
4. Shell spins into a striped pneumatic tube/trail aimed at a Capitol vignette.
5. Capitol receives the shell with a comic stamp/chime.
6. Display completed-round message, then transition automatically.

### Escape

1. Freeze the scene after the tenth miss.
2. Money bags pop around Mitch with deliberately impossible timing.
3. Helicopter with flag and cockpit passenger enters; rotor/wash animate.
4. Rope lowers; hook/loop connects to Mitch and bags.
5. Bundle rises and helicopter flies offscreen.
6. Show **MITCH GOT AWAY** card, run statistics, best score, and Search Again.

## Required Published Disclaimer

Use the approved wording from `docs/GAME-DESIGN.md` on the title screen and in Credits. It must be
visible without opening a legal modal. Copy must identify the work as fictional, exaggerated
political satire and state that it is unaffiliated with depicted people or institutions.

## Human Approval Gates

Before this phase is considered complete, the owner reviews the Turtle Mitch rig, Elaine cockpit
rig, Chinese flag treatment, title/disclaimer, both cutscenes, and all public copy in motion.
